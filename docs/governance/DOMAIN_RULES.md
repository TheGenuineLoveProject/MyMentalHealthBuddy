# Domain Rules

## Healing Domain
Purpose: education, reflection, self-awareness, emotional literacy, guided practice.
Rules:
- No diagnosis.
- No therapy replacement claims.
- No crisis automation without human-centered escalation.
- No upsells inside crisis or vulnerable-state flows.

## Business Domain
Purpose: subscriptions, billing, products, admin monetization.
Rules:
- Must stay separate from healing safety flows.
- Stripe events must be verified.
- Pricing must be transparent.

## Platform Domain
Purpose: deployment, reliability, backups, observability, security.
Rules:
- Health gates must pass before launch.
- Backup and restore must be verified.
- Production blockers must stop deployment.

## Design Domain
Purpose: brand cohesion, accessibility, usability.
Rules:
- Must meet readable contrast.
- Must support mobile.
- Must use approved design tokens.

## Research Domain
Purpose: evidence registry, educational quality, outcome tracking.
Rules:
- Evidence level must be labeled.
- Uncertainty must be disclosed.
- Recommendations must be measurable.
